Settings

The address and the sender are checked before anything goes out.

Sender must be on mail.salonvine.com. Booking confirmations and receipts go out from salonvine.com; if this outreach ever gets marked as spam, that reputation must not touch them. Add mail.salonvine.com as a domain in Resend, put its DNS records into Netlify DNS, and wait for the green tick. Until then, “Switch on” refuses.

Send yourself a test

Exactly what a salon owner would get, delivered to any inbox you choose. Do this before switching on.

Add salons to the list

Paste a CSV. It needs an email column; name, salon and city columns are picked up if they exist. Duplicates and anyone who already unsubscribed are skipped automatically.
